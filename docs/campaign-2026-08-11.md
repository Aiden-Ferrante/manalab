*One day's arc, kept because the sequence is the argument: every architectural

## Part 1 — the database made exact

**Loading was never the gap; reading was.** All 35,142 playable cards load (the
3,484 excluded from the 38,623-line bulk file are 2,676 art-series and 808
tokens, verified against the raw file — no card has a null `type_line`). The gap
was faces: Scryfall nulls top-level `oracle_text` whenever `card_faces` is
present, so **886 cards — every transform, adventure, split, modal DFC, prepare
and flip — were silently textless**, and the coverage measurement counted every
one as a *success* because a card with no clauses cannot fail one.

The fixes, in dependency order, each with the number it moved:

| Step | Commit | Effect on the headline |
|---|---|---|
| Coverage counts unread ≠ vanilla | `e41e6b2` | 63.4% → **60.9%** (honest drop) |
| `faces` table, one row per printed side | `c873aad` | 36,030 faces, 0 orphans |
| Parser reads every face under its own name | `8fec718` | 60.9% → **61.8%** (886 unread → 0) |
| Fetch keyed on provenance, not existence | `fb40c45` | a silent 3-day-stale no-op became "downloaded / already current" |
| Exhaustive verification, not sampled | `0c5fd53` | 35,142 cards × every field vs source: **0 diffs** |

The derived fields got the check comparison cannot give: every disagreement
between our `produces` and Scryfall's `produced_mana` is one of two documented
designs (colors-only filtering for the payment solver; fetchland inference), and
**zero source-attested producers are invisible to the evaluator**. The Karsten
validation reproduces the published colored-source tables end to end — the
strongest external check available, exercising parsing, mulligans, sequencing
and payment at once. `examples/verify_exact.py` pins all of it; exit 0 means
exact.

**Classification** (`f478394`): every face's type line split into supertypes /
card types / subtypes with unknowns *recorded, never guessed* — 9 unrecognised
words in 36,030 faces, all Un-set jokes. Landed in the database as `face_types`
so optimization groups are one `GROUP BY` away, and on the model as
`Card.card_types` (union across faces: Brazen Borrower counts as a creature
*and* an instant, deliberately).

**Parser precision** (`b5f1a4f`, `5212d9d`): the gap list worked top-down
(61.9% → 66.9% of cards), then a systematic smell-hunt over all 60k effects
found the two wrong-parse classes — quoted grants read as the card's own text,
ability words swallowing their triggers — and killed both (smells 4.83% →
2.11%, coverage honestly *down* to 65.6% as fake successes left the column).
The `X → -1` sentinel died the same day; the test suite had been asserting the
exact behaviour its own docstring forbade. A 30-card human-audit file with every
historically treacherous card kind seeded awaits marking
(`reports/audit_effects.md`); generating it surfaced planeswalker loyalty costs
dropping silently (U+2212 ≠ a brace cost) — the top of the next precision queue.

## Part 2 — the search ladder

`search-problem.md` stated the problem (a multiset on a ~10⁶⁰-point lattice
polytope; a stochastic simulator behind the seam; five structural properties
each licensing a framework) and one load-bearing hypothesis: **variance in f is
mostly compositional**. Instead of arguing it, we measured it — three times,
once per objective rung, same design each time (48 compositions × 6 identities,
300 CRN games, noise floor measured on a second seed and subtracted):

| Rung | Objective | Composition share | What the paired search run showed |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `consistency_t4` | **86.6%** | climb pinned lands to the 32-rail, hollowed 4-drops — the objective pays for mana *available* and nothing a spell does |
| 2 | `throughput` (= mana spent) | **67.7%** | interior land optimum found (26–27); climb honestly *beaten* by a hand-picked top-heavy comp — 80 first-improvement proposals stall short |
| 3 | `throughput+synergy@0.5` | **46.4%** | climb beat every hand-picked comp (23.46 vs 23.21), lands interior at 25 |

Supporting machinery built along the way, each a permanent fixture:

- **Bilevel mana fitting** (`6a0609f`): `fit_basics` — pip-proportional basics
  with largest-remainder exactness, a min-one splash guarantee, Wastes for
  pipless decks — behind `FittedEvaluator`, so no optimizer anywhere in the
  stack can spend simulation budget on a mana base the hypergeometric already
  determines.
- **Synergy ports** (`5948e59`, `3ebbf60`): provide/require interfaces read
  mechanically off parsed effects, keywords, costs and triggers. The corpus
  census shows every port two-sided and the aristocrats chain fully populated
  (3,122 token producers · 1,971 sac outlets · 1,264 death payoffs — and 260
  cards on two legs at once). `synergy_score = Σ min(providers, consumers)` per
  port — a bottleneck reading, supermodular exactly where combos live —
  injected as a zero-variance metric so every statistical tool downstream works
  unchanged. λ = 0.5 is a **declared prior**, calibrated when an evaluator can
  watch a sacrifice actually resolve. The `mana` port is deliberately excluded
  from the score: throughput already pays for ramp, and paying twice re-opens
  the big-mana rail rung 2 closed.

**The finding that decides the architecture** (ADR 0007): identity's variance
share moved 13.4% → 32.3% → 53.6% as the objective learned to see what spells
do — monotone, predicted in advance each time, and measured under CRN with the
noise floor subtracted. The two-level design (composition simplex, then
identity assignment) stands, but the hypergraph column is load-bearing *now*,
not later.

## What is deliberately not claimed

- **Parse coverage is 65.6%, and every synergy edge inherits that bound.**
  Ports on unparsed clauses do not exist here. Bounded and known beats implicit.
- **λ is a prior, not a measurement.** The engine (M5) is what turns closed
  pairs into win-rate deltas.
- **Every objective so far is gameable, including the current one** — mana
  spent ≈ cmc still overvalues expensive spells; the synergy term corrects
  composition, not card quality. The ladder continues on purpose: each rung's
  gameable edge was predicted before its run, found by its run, and closed by
  the next rung. That loop *is* the method.
