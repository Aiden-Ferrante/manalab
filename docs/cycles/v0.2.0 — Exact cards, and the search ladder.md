## Problem

A failure that had already happened, twice over. The database was **silently
wrong**: 886 cards — every transform, adventure, split, modal DFC — had no
reachable text because Scryfall nulls top-level `oracle_text` when `card_faces`
exists, and the coverage measurement counted each one as a parsing *success*.
And the deck-search question was about to be decided by taste: hypergraphs
versus simplexes, argued instead of measured.

## Appetite

One day. Held — the cycle cut the same day it opened.

## In scope

- Faces: every printed side loaded, parsed under its own name, verified.
- Exactness: the whole corpus checked against the source, not sampled.
- Classification: type lines split and queryable in SQL.
- Parser precision: the wrong-parse classes found systematically and closed.
- The search problem stated precisely, and its architecture decided by a
  standing experiment across an objective ladder.

## Guardrails (derived from the problem, not invented)

- A number that got worse honestly beats one that stayed wrong: coverage was
  *allowed to drop* twice (63.4→60.9, 66.9→65.6) as fake successes left it.
- Every comparison CRN-paired; the noise floor measured, never assumed.
- Nothing writes to the chart or another repo; explicit-path commits only.

## Retro

**What worked.** Measure-before-argue, three times: the variance experiment
settled in an afternoon what could have been debated for a month, and each
objective's gameable edge was predicted before its run found it. The seam paid
for itself — three evaluator wrappers composed without the simulator noticing.

**What dragged.** My own labels: the "modern pool" that never was, an inflated
face count in ADR 0006, test arithmetic wrong twice. Every one was caught by
rereading output against claims, none by the type system — the lesson is that
labels are part of the experiment and need the same verification as data.

**Was the appetite honest?** Yes, barely — documentation landed after the last
experiment with no slack. A two-arc day is the ceiling, not the norm.

**Concepts → Learn Hub.** Variance decomposition / ANOVA with a measured noise
floor; supermodularity as the signature of complementarity; bottleneck (min)
readings of flow; CRN as a design surface, not a trick.
